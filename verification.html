The Zero-Mock Empirical Mandate

Laboratory Quality & Verification Protocol

TRDL enforces a strict institutional policy prohibiting simulated benchmark outcomes, placeholder telemetry, and synthesized experimental outputs.

•
Empirical Computing: All computational performance metrics, FLOP counts, autograd execution timings, and benchmark scores must be collected from physical CPUs, GPUs, or edge hardware instances.
•
Biochemical Traceability: Wet-lab enzyme assays, CFU survival counts, and micro-encapsulation stability data must follow Department of Biotechnology (DBT) and BIRAC traceability requirements.
•
Algorithmic Integrity: Theoretical claims must be accompanied by explicit derivations or reproducible open scripts without unverified black-box assumptions.

Public Procurement & Tendering Standing

TRDL actively participates in Indian public procurement tenders, contract scientific services, and defense innovation challenges across national portals.

STATUTORY BIDDER PRIVILEGE // GFR RULE 170 COMPLIANCE

Pursuant to Rule 170 of the General Financial Rules (GFR) 2017 and the Public Procurement Policy for MSEs Order 2012, Tewary Research and Development Lab (TRDL) participates in all Indian public procurement and national institute tenders with 100% exemption from Earnest Money Deposits (EMD) and Tender Document Fees under verified Udyam Registration (UDYAM-JH-0010572).

NATIONAL PORTAL

Central Public Procurement Portal (CPPP)

Bidding on open competitive tenders issued by CSIR labs (CDRI, CCMB, IIIM), ICMR institutes, and national universities for contract research, specialized bio-analysis, and custom chemical synthesis.

GOV e-MARKETPLACE
