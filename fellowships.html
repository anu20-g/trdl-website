Sovereign Research Fellowships

TRDL inducts promising student researchers into focused, project-bounded research fellowships. Fellows receive full lab resources, computing access, and guidance to execute concrete technical work packages under strict scientific discipline.

01

Fellowship Governance & Operating Principles

1. Project-Bounded Tenure

Fellowship terms remain active throughout the execution of assigned research work packages. Progression is determined strictly by verified empirical deliverables, scientific ethics, and reproducibility.

2. Full Resource Provisioning

TRDL supplies and funds verified computational hardware, software toolchains, laboratory consumables, and analytical testing needed to execute assigned projects.

3. Team Induction Authority

Active fellows are authorized to assemble technical teams and student investigators under their scientific coordination to execute assigned work packages.

4. Retained Inventorship

While core IP vests institutionally in TRDL for sovereign protection and commercial administration via TRDL-IAC, fellows are formally credited as Named Co-Inventors on all resulting patent disclosures.
